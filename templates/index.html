When to Use Gemini 3.8 TTS vs. Chirp 3 HD

          
    
            
  • 
              Gemini 3.8 TTS (gemini-3.8-flash-tts / lite):
              Best for seamless intra-sentence code-switching across 130 languages, expressive acting via speechMetadata.style, inline vocal pauses (<short pause>), and continuous speaker prosody using dual-track respelling.
            
    • 
            
  • 
              Chirp 3 HD (<locale>-Chirp3-HD-<Voice>):
              Best when strict phoneme-level governance is required via CustomPronunciations (Greco IPA / X-SAMPA for es-* & pt-BR; tone-numbered Pinyin for cmn-CN) or SSML <phoneme>, <say-as>, and <voice> tags.
